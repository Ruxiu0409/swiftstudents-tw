With taps, drags, long presses, pinches and multi-touch gestures, you simulate the key steps of chipmaking: Wet Clean, Deposition, Lithography, Etching, Ion Implantation, Diffusion and Chemical-Mechanical Planarization (CMP), then Chip Probing, Dicing, Die Attach, Wire Bonding, Molding and Final Test. Every stage explains the process behind it, gives gesture hints and shows the result visually, so a complex manufacturing technology becomes intuitive and easy to follow.
