The main screen shows a house with five rooms, and each level has its own energy-saving tasks: switch off the desk lamp; water the plant, then turn off the air conditioner; turn off the shower and water heater; close the fridge and turn off the dining-table light and oven; and switch off the fan and floor lamp before heading out. The name "Camel" comes from a friend joking that Chun-Yu looks like a camel. Next, Chun-Yu hopes to connect the game to real home appliances so players can monitor and cut their household energy use right from the game.
