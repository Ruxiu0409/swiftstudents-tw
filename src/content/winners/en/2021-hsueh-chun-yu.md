Built around the theme "Everyone is friend," Hello World promotes racial and gender equality. Players enter an emoji, then scan the floor or a tabletop with AR, and characters of different skin tones and genders appear and greet them, each in their own way, as a gentle lesson in accepting one another.

ARKit and SpriteKit project the 2D characters into the camera view, while SwiftUI and UIKit handle the interaction, all packaged as a Playground Book presented in levels. With no prior AR or Playground Book experience, Chun-Yu learned along the way over two to three months. The characters were first planned in 3D but became 2D because of technical limits, and the first version includes four kinds of greetings.
